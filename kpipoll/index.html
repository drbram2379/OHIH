Poll rules · method · sources

How the poll decides the 2027 Group KPI

The poll turns the five track briefs from the 2026 OHIH KPI and Program Workshop (pages 24–28) into one Group OHIH KPI set for 2027. The result goes to the OHS GM for confirmation; each site then builds its KPI sheets and programs on it, signed by 15 December.

Who votes

Fifteen named participants, three per site: the OH doctor, the IH officer or OHS system lead, and the OHS manager. PGM's IH slot (Samuel L / Daud N) is one ballot.

How to vote

  • Choose your site and your name.
  • Pick exactly 3 KPIs in each of the five tracks. Each option shows its 2026 baseline, source, frequency and the harm it prevents, from the KPI dictionary.
  • Optional: the program block your site would run first, your site's maturity on the track (1–4), and a note with your site baseline, 2027 target or another KPI.
  • You can change your ballot until the poll closes.

How the result is counted

    Who sees what

    • Everyone sees who has voted.
    • Results open to you after you submit your ballot, and to everyone when the poll closes, so each vote stays independent.
    • Ballots are not secret: the facilitator sees each ballot to check that each person voted once.
    • The poll holds names, roles and choices only. It holds no worker health data.

    Sources

    • Track briefs, KPI options, facts and program blocks: workshop deck pages 24–28.
    • Baselines, sources, frequencies and track weights (30/25/20/15/10): the 2027 KPI dictionary in presentation 3.
    • Presentation 3 listed 19 candidate KPIs. The workshop narrows each track to 3, which gives 15 Group KPIs. The options not selected stay available as site KPIs.
    • "Score 1 findings closed" has no dictionary entry yet. Its baseline and source were filled in by the consultant team; confirm them at the results review.
    • Timeline: the workshop "Next steps" page. The poll steps are proposed.
